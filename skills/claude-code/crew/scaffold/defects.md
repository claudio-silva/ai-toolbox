# Crew defects

Leaf defects that reviews and QA found and the lead deferred: low or medium severity, nothing pending builds on them, and they fail safely. Briefs list the ones on a task's surfaces so agents neither fix nor chase them; one that blocks or breaks other work is promoted and fixed at once. The set's deferred defects are fixed before its final review. Kept by `.crew/bin/crew defect`.
